# OriTune Privacy Policy

**Application Name**: OriTune (OriTune - MP3 Converter)  
**Developer**: Orion (`@orion00`)  
**Developer Contact**: `orion00.kwon@gmail.com`

### 1. Collection of Personal Information
OriTune ("the App") **does not collect, store, or transmit** any personally identifiable information (such as name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers).

### 2. Device Permissions and Purpose of Use
The App requests only the minimum necessary permissions required to provide its core media conversion and audio playback features:
- **Storage / Audio Permission (`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`)**: Used solely to access local media files selected by the user for conversion and to save the converted MP3 files to the user's chosen folder.
- **Internet Permission (`INTERNET`)**: Used solely for retrieving media stream metadata and downloading user-requested content.
- All conversion and encoding processes are performed entirely **locally on your device**. No personal files or data are ever transmitted to external servers without your consent.

### 3. Third-Party Sharing and External Libraries
The App does not sell, lease, trade, or share user data with any third parties. Open-source libraries used by the app (such as FFmpeg) operate strictly locally on the device for audio encoding.

### 4. Children's Privacy
The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in app updates or posted at this URL.

### 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via email at `orion00.kwon@gmail.com`.
